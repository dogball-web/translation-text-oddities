Room 353 — Notes for Classroom Use

Language Laboratory / Internal Notes

  1. Keep the red curtains fully closed during class. This reduces screen glare and visual distraction.
  2. Check the curtains before using the projector.
  3. If you see someone in the classroom across the courtyard, check whether the same person is still inside Room 353.
  4. If they are, close the curtain.
  5. Do not ask the person to look outside.
  6. Do not tell them who you saw.
  7. If the person outside notices you first, do not wave back.

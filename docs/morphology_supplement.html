Morpheme Structure｜補充例句（掃描整理）

形式一般功能例子
re-again / backrewrite, reread, rebuild
un-not / reverse a state or actionunhappy, untie, unlock
mis-wrongly / incorrectly / in the wrong placemisread, mislabel, misplace
-erperson or thing performing an actionteacher, writer
-nessstate or qualitykindness, darkness
Example: misplace = put something in the wrong place.
